## Industry Pain Points

### Property Management
1. **Tenant Inquiry Response** - High volume, slow follow-up = lost leads
2. **Maintenance Coordination** - Vendor routing, status updates, owner communication
3. **Owner Reporting** - Monthly financials, occupancy data (manual compilation)
4. **Lease Renewals** - 60/90 day campaigns, e-signature workflows
5. **Showing Coordination** - Manual scheduling, back-and-forth emails

**Existing Tools:** AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager (underutilized)
**Automation Opportunity:** Custom workflows on top of existing PMS

---

### Med Spas
1. **Appointment Booking** - Phone/DM volume, manual scheduling
2. **No-Show Prevention** - Missed appointments cost $500+ per slot
3. **Post-Appointment Follow-Up** - Rebooking, product recommendations
4. **Lead Nurture** - Consultation → treatment conversion
5. **Membership Management** - Renewal tracking, payment automation

**Automation Opportunity:** Self-scheduling + SMS reminders + rebooking campaigns

---

### Home Services (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical)
1. **Inbound Lead Response** - Missed calls = lost jobs
2. **Appointment Scheduling** - Manual back-and-forth
3. **Follow-Up** - Estimates, seasonal maintenance, referrals
4. **Review Generation** - Asking for Google/Yelp reviews
5. **Seasonal Campaigns** - HVAC tune-ups, water heater flushes

**Automation Opportunity:** Missed call text-back + self-scheduling + review sequences

## Discovery Question Framework

### Current State (Understand Operations)
1. "Walk me through what happens when a new lead/inquiry comes in."
2. "What tools are you using to manage [core operation]?"
3. "How do you handle [key pain point]?"
4. "What does a typical day look like for you?"

### Pain Identification
5. "Where are you spending the most manual time each week?"
6. "What's the most frustrating part of [their business]?"
7. "How often do you miss opportunities due to slow response?"
8. "What happens when you're out of the office?"

### Goal Setting
9. "If you could automate one thing tomorrow, what would it be?"
10. "What would 10 extra hours/week free you up to do?"
11. "What's your goal for the business this year?"

### Decision Context
12. "Have you looked at automation tools before? What stopped you?"
13. "What would make a solution worth investing in?"
14. "Who else needs to be involved in this decision?"

## Technical Stack Knowledge

### CRM Platforms
- **Dubsado:** Best for service-based businesses (property mgmt, med spa)
- **HoneyBook:** Creative industry favorite (photographers, event planners)
- **Go High Level:** All-in-one (more complex, higher learning curve)

### Scheduling Tools
- **Calendly:** Simple, clean UI, good integrations
- **Acuity:** More features, customization options
- **Calendar.com:** Newer, modern interface

### Email Automation
- **ConvertKit:** Creator-focused, simple sequences
- **ActiveCampaign:** More robust, CRM built-in
- **Mailchimp:** Widely known, less powerful automation

### Landing Pages
- **Carrd:** Fast, simple, cheap
- **Webflow:** More complex, professional designs
- **Leadpages:** Conversion-focused, templates
