**Why they're gold:**
- Drowning in repetitive tasks: tenant inquiries, maintenance requests, rent collection, lease renewals, owner reporting
- Most use outdated software (AppFolio, Buildium) with manual gaps

- High willingness to pay (they charge 8-12% of rent, have budget)

**Specific pain points to target:**
- Tenant inquiry response (nights/weekends)
- Maintenance request routing and follow-up
- Move-in/move-out scheduling and checklists
- Owner monthly reporting
- Lease renewal reminders and document generation

## Recommended First Target: Property Management

**Here's why:**

1. **Clear ROI math**: "You manage 100 units. Each tenant inquiry takes 10 minutes. You get 50/week. That's 8 hours/week just responding to inquiries. I can cut that to 30 minutes."

2. **Quantifiable pain**: Hours spent, response times, missed renewals, late rent—all measurable.

3. **Your background fits**: Systems design, automation, data flows—this is bread and butter.

4. **No Jack dependency**: Unlike fitness, you can attack this without waiting for relationship development.

5. **High density in Austin**: 50+ firms, growing market, lots of out-of-state investors needing local management.

6. **Referral potential**: Property managers know each other. One case study = 5 warm intros.

## Discovery Call Questions by Industry

### Property Management
1. "Walk me through what happens when a tenant submits a maintenance request at 10pm on Saturday."
2. "How do you currently handle lease renewal reminders?"
3. "What does your owner reporting process look like? How long does it take?"
4. "When's the last time a good tenant left because of slow response time?"

### Med Spas
1. "How do you remind clients when their Botox is wearing off?"
2. "What happens after a new client's first treatment?"
3. "How do you collect reviews from happy clients?"
4. "What's your no-show rate? How do you handle confirmations?"

### Home Services
1. "When an emergency call comes in after hours, what happens next?"
2. "How many quotes do you send out vs. how many convert? What's your follow-up process?"
3. "How do you remind customers about annual maintenance?"
4. "What does your tech's day look like in terms of paperwork?"
