### Pre-Meeting Research (Do before coffee)

**Property Management Pain Points to Validate:**

| Pain Point | Discovery Question |
|------------|-------------------|
| Tenant inquiries | "When a tenant texts at 10pm about a leaky faucet, what happens next?" |
| Maintenance coordination | "Walk me through a maintenance request from start to finish." |
| Owner reporting | "How do you keep property owners updated? How long does that take?" |
| Lease renewals | "How do you track when leases are coming up? What's the renewal process?" |
| Rent collection | "What happens when rent is late? How much time does collections take?" |
| Tenant screening | "What does your tenant screening workflow look like?" |
| Move-in/move-out | "How do you handle move-in inspections and scheduling?" |

**Real Estate Brokerage Pain Points:**

| Pain Point | Discovery Question |
|------------|-------------------|
| Lead follow-up | "When a new lead comes in from Zillow or your website, what happens?" |
| Past client nurture | "How do you stay in touch with past clients for referrals?" |
| Listing coordination | "What's the most time-consuming part of getting a new listing live?" |
| Showing scheduling | "How do you handle showing requests?" |
| Transaction management | "From accepted offer to close, what eats up the most time?" |

**CDRE-Specific (Divorce Real Estate):**

| Pain Point | Discovery Question |
|------------|-------------------|
| High-touch clients | "Divorce clients are probably more emotionally demanding — how do you manage that communication load?" |
| Multi-party coordination | "You're dealing with two parties, two attorneys, etc. How do you keep everyone aligned?" |

### During the Coffee Meeting

**Agenda (30 min):**

0-5 min: Rapport, catch up on property
5-20 min: Discovery questions (listen 80%, talk 20%)
20-25 min: "What would you wave a magic wand and fix?"
25-30 min: Soft close (see below)

**Soft Close Script:**

If she mentions a clear pain point:

> "That's really interesting — the [maintenance coordination / lead follow-up / owner reporting] thing sounds like it eats up a ton of time. I'm actually building a system for my wife's business right now that automates exactly that kind of stuff. Once I'm done in a couple weeks, would you be open to me showing you what's possible? No commitment — you'd basically be helping me refine my offer, and if it's useful, great. If not, no worries."

If no clear pain point emerges:

> "This has been super helpful. As I build out my business, would it be okay if I checked back in with you in a few weeks? I'd love to show you what I'm working on and get your feedback."

**What you're listening for:**

- [ ] Specific tasks that take too long
- [ ] Things that "fall through the cracks"
- [ ] Frustration words: "always," "never," "hate," "annoying"
- [ ] Manual processes that should be automated
- [ ] Tools she wishes existed
- [ ] How many properties she manages (scale of pain)
