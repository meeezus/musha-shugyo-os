Alex Hillman's automated workflow using bird CLI (Twitter CLI) to process bookmarks into a knowledge base with AI agent assistance.

## Workflow Steps

1. Bookmark a tweet
2. Bird grabs new bookmarks every 60 seconds
3. Agent reads the tweet and depending on contents adds to a queue of things to review, try, or simply add to knowledge base

## Capabilities

- Auto saves links, podcasts, YouTube videos, etc.
- Includes transcripts and quotes that would be useful or interesting
- Auto suggests ways to integrate ideas and open source projects into the JFDI system
- Compounds over time
