**Hormozi says:** Start with warm outreach before cold. Use your existing network first.

> "Warm outreach is the fastest way to make money because these people already know you."

**Our plan:** Reaching out to Theresa Bastian first — an existing business relationship.

### 2. Grand Slam Offer Structure (From $100M Offers)

**Hormozi's Value Equation:**
```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```

**Our Freedom Engine Offer:**
- Dream Outcome: 15+ hours/week saved ✅
- Likelihood: Triple guarantee (results, function, experience) ✅
- Time Delay: 14-day delivery ✅
- Effort: "No tech overwhelm" promise ✅

**Value Stack:** 12 components totaling $14,400 value for $8,500 ✅

✅ **ALIGNED** — We built the offer using Hormozi's framework in our previous session.

## ⚠️ GAPS TO FIX

### Gap 1: No Lead Magnet for Property Managers

**Hormozi says ($100M Leads):**
> "Engage your leads with lead magnets. Give them something valuable for free first."

**Current plan:** We go straight from outreach to discovery call.

**FIX:** Create a simple lead magnet for property managers:
- "The 5 Admin Tasks Eating 15+ Hours/Week (And How to Automate Them)"
- Free audit checklist
- Quick video walkthrough

**Add to Week 1:** Create 1-page PDF lead magnet for property managers.

---

### Gap 2: No Clear "Free Goodwill" Offer

**Hormozi says ($100M Offers & Leads):**
> "Free Goodwill: Do something valuable for free to create reciprocity."

**Current plan:** We're asking Theresa for her time but not offering value upfront.

**FIX:** Before or during the coffee, offer something tangible:
- Quick audit of her current tech stack
- 3 specific automation ideas for her business
- A template or checklist she can use immediately

**Update Theresa outreach:** Add a "gift" angle:
> "I've been researching property management workflows and put together a quick checklist of the top automation opportunities. Happy to share it over coffee."

---

### Gap 3: No Upsell/Downsell/Continuity Path

**Hormozi says ($100M Money Models):**
> Four offer types: Attraction → Upsell → Downsell → Continuity

**Current plan:** We have the core Freedom Engine ($8,500) but the ascension ladder is weak.

**FIX:** Clarify the complete money model:

| Type | Offer | Price |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Attraction** | Free Systems Audit | $0 |
| **Core** | Freedom Engine | $8,500 |
| **Upsell** | Freedom Engine + Growth Package | $15,000 |
| **Downsell** | Systems Audit Only | $500 |
| **Continuity** | Monthly Optimization Retainer | $1,500/mo |

**Add to Week 1:** Document the full money model.

---

### Gap 4: No Referral System Designed

**Hormozi says ($100M Leads):**
> "Customer referrals are the highest converting leads. Design your referral system."

**Current plan:** We mention "ask for referrals" but no system.

**FIX:** Create a referral offer:
- $500 referral bonus for Theresa if she refers a closed client
- "Founding Partner" program for early clients who refer
- Template email/text she can forward

**Add to Week 2:** Design formal referral program before closing first client.

---

### Gap 5: Weak Scarcity/Urgency

**Hormozi says ($100M Offers):**
> "Scarcity and urgency are ethical when real. Limit your capacity."

**Current plan:** We say "3 clients/month" but it's not woven into discovery.

**FIX:** Make the scarcity tangible in the discovery call:
> "I only work with 3 clients per month because each build takes my full attention for 2 weeks. I've got one slot left for January."
