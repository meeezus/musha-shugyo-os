# I. YOUR CURRENT ROUTINE (MAPPED TO FIVE PILLARS)

## 6:30 AM — Wake, Hydrate, Sunlight, Gratitude
**→ MISOGI (Purification)**  
- Hydration = cleansing the body after sleep
- Sunlight = circadian alignment (nature's signal)
- Gratitude = mental purification (clear mind before action)

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of sunrise light exposure (within 30 min of waking)
- 90 consecutive days of hydration ritual (16-32 oz water immediately)

---

## 7:00 AM — Meditation + Visualization
**→ MUSHIN/ZANSHIN (Mind)**  
- Meditation = training no-mind (mushin)
- Visualization = sustained awareness (zanshin—seeing the day before it unfolds)

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of meditation (even if just 5 min)
- Build to 20+ min sessions by Day 90
- Track: How many days did you enter flow (time disappeared)?

---

## 8:00 AM — Morning Movement / Mobility
**→ KEIKO (Practice/Body Prep)**  
- Mobility = foundational movement ("how to breathe and walk")
- Not the main training, but essential prep

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of intentional movement (even 10 min counts)
- Full squat (ass-to-grass) hold for 2 minutes by Day 90
- Touch toes without bending knees by Day 90

---

## 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM — Sacred Work Block (Writing, Editing, Creating)
**→ DŌ (The Path)**  
- Creation as practice
- Your "dōjō" is the blank page
- This is where you forge your craft

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of sacred work block (even if just 60 min)
- 30 consecutive days of 2-hour unbroken focus (no interruptions)
- 12 Scrolls written (1/week for 90 days = ~12-13 total)
- Track: How many flow sessions (time disappeared)?

---

## 12:00 PM — Training (Anabolic Focus)
**→ KEIKO (Practice/Body)**  
- The main physical forging
- Strength, martial arts, or endurance
- Intensity as ritual

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of training logged (even light days count)
- 60+ days of "serious" training (4-5x/week for ~12 weeks)
- Strength benchmark progress:
  - Bench: +10-20 lbs from baseline
  - Squat: +20-30 lbs from baseline
  - Deadlift: +30-40 lbs from baseline
- OR martial arts: 100+ mat hours logged (BJJ, Krav Maga, weapons)

---

## 2:00 PM — Recovery + Meal
**→ KEIKO (continued) + MISOGI (Refueling)**  
- Recovery is part of training (sleep, rest, nutrition)
- Seasonal eating = environmental alignment

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of post-training meal (within 1 hour of training)
- 80%+ whole foods (track weekly—aim for 6+ days/week)
- 1 x 24-hour fast/month (3 fasts total over 90 days)

---

## 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM — Content Creation / Coaching Prep
**→ DŌ (The Path continued)**  
- Documenting the practice
- Teaching to learn
- Outputting what you've lived

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of documentation (even if just journaling)
- 12 pieces of public content (repurposed from Scrolls, or standalone)
- 4 teaching moments (coaching calls, even if free/informal)

---

## 6:00 PM — Dinner Before Sunset
**→ MISOGI (Circadian Alignment)**  
- Eating with the sun = honoring natural cycles
- No screens = presence

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 80% adherence to dinner before sunset (72+ days out of 90)
- Track sunset time daily (it shifts—adjust accordingly)

---

## 8:00 PM — Family or Solitude
**→ GIRI/ON (Service/Relationships)**  
- Presence with family = honoring duty
- Solitude = necessary for introverts (also a practice)
- Phone away = full attention

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of phone-free evenings (from 8 PM onward)
- 90% adherence to family presence (81+ evenings fully present)
- Weekly family ritual established (dinner, game night, walk—whatever fits)

---

## 9:30 PM — Reflection Journal
**→ INTEGRATION OF ALL FIVE PILLARS**  
- Daily accountability: "Did I show up?"
- Not judgment—calibration

**90-Day Goal:**  
- 90 consecutive days of journaling (even if just 3 sentences)
- Answer these questions daily:
  1. **Keiko:** Did I train today? How did my body feel?
  2. **Misogi:** Did I honor nature (light, cold, food)? Did I stay off screens?
  3. **Mushin:** Did I meditate? Did I enter flow?
  4. **Dō:** Did I create today? What did I learn?
  5. **Giri/On:** Was I present with my family? Did I serve someone?

# III. THE 90-DAY BREAKDOWN (PHASE BY PHASE)

## Month 1 (Days 1-30): FOUNDATION

**Goal:** Establish the routine. Don't chase perfection—chase consistency.

**Focus:**
- Lock in the morning ritual (wake, hydrate, sunlight, meditation)
- Lock in the evening ritual (family presence, journal)
- Everything else: just show up (even if imperfect)

**Key Milestones:**
- [ ] 30 consecutive days of morning routine (wake → meditate)
- [ ] 30 consecutive days of evening journal
- [ ] 4 Scrolls written (1/week)
- [ ] 20+ training sessions logged (4-5x/week)

**What Success Looks Like:**
- You don't skip the bookends (morning + evening)
- The middle of the day is messy—that's fine
- You're learning what works for YOUR body/schedule

---

## Month 2 (Days 31-60): REFINEMENT

**Goal:** Increase intensity and duration. Now that the routine exists, deepen it.

**Focus:**
- Extend meditation from 5 min → 10-15 min
- Extend deep work from 60 min → 90-120 min
- Add cold exposure (if not doing it yet)
- Add tech boundaries (phone-free mornings/evenings)

**Key Milestones:**
- [ ] 30 consecutive days of 10+ min meditation
- [ ] 30 consecutive days of 90+ min deep work
- [ ] 4 more Scrolls written (total: 8)
- [ ] 20+ training sessions (total: 40+)
- [ ] 1 x 24-hour fast completed

**What Success Looks Like:**
- The routine feels easier (less willpower needed)
- You catch yourself slipping and return faster
- You're documenting insights (patterns emerge)

---

## Month 3 (Days 61-90): EMBODIMENT

**Goal:** This is YOU now. The practice is integrated.

**Focus:**
- Test yourself (can you hold the standards?)
- Push intensity (PR in the gym, write your best Scroll)
- Prepare to teach (you've earned the right)

**Key Milestones:**
- [ ] 30 consecutive days of 20+ min meditation (total: 90 days meditating)
- [ ] 30 consecutive days of 120+ min deep work
- [ ] 4 more Scrolls written (total: 12)
- [ ] 20+ training sessions (total: 60+)
- [ ] 1 more 24-hour fast (total: 2-3 fasts)
- [ ] 90 consecutive days of evening journal (complete)

**What Success Looks Like:**
- You don't think about the routine—you just do it
- You have 12 Scrolls (proof of Dō)
- You have 60+ training logs (proof of Keiko)
- You have 90 journal entries (proof of Reckon)
- You're ready to teach because you've lived it

# V. CONTINGENCY PLANS (WHEN LIFE HAPPENS)

## If You Miss a Day

**Don't break the chain twice.**

You missed today. Fine.  
Don't miss tomorrow.

Journal why you missed:
- Was it unavoidable (sick, emergency)?
- Was it avoidable (lazy, distracted)?

If avoidable: What will you do differently tomorrow?

---

## If You Miss a Week

**Don't quit. Recalibrate.**

Life happened. You fell off.  
Don't restart the 90 days—just return.

Ask:
- Which pillar did I neglect? (Keiko, Misogi, Mushin, Dō, Giri/On?)
- Why did I neglect it? (Too ambitious? Wrong timing? Lack of support?)
- How do I simplify? (Cut volume, not frequency—do LESS but DAILY)

Then get back on the path.

---

## If You're Overwhelmed

**Focus on the bookends only:**

**Morning (Non-negotiable):**
- Wake at 6:30 AM
- Hydrate + sunlight
- 5 min meditation

**Evening (Non-negotiable):**
- Phone away at 8 PM
- Journal for 3 sentences

Everything else: do your best.

Once the bookends are locked in for 30 days, add back the middle.

# VI. WEEKLY REVIEW RITUAL (EVERY SUNDAY)

## The Sunday Reckon (30-60 min)

**Step 1: Score the week**  
Fill in your weekly scorecard (Keiko, Misogi, Mushin, Dō, Giri/On)

**Step 2: Reflect**  
Answer these questions:
1. What worked this week?
2. What didn't work?
3. Where did I lose presence? (When did I drift?)
4. What's one thing I'll adjust next week?

**Step 3: Plan**  
- Write next week's Scroll topic (which pillar will you explore?)
- Schedule training sessions (lock them in calendar)
- Identify one person to serve (family, friend, mentee)

**Step 4: Gratitude**  
Write 3 things you're grateful for from this week.
