# Austin Property Management Industry - Market Intelligence

**Last Updated:** January 1, 2026
**Source:** Research for Automation Agency launch

---

## Industry Overview

### Market Size
- 50+ property management firms in Austin metro
- Growing market due to out-of-state investors
- Typical firm manages 50-500 properties
- Industry standard: 8-12% of monthly rent as management fee

### Technology Landscape
- Most firms use: AppFolio, Buildium, or Propertyware
- Common gaps: After-hours tenant communication, maintenance coordination, owner reporting
- Low automation adoption beyond basic property management software

---

## Pain Points (Validated)

### High Priority
1. **Tenant Inquiry Response** - Nights/weekends inquiries create constant interruptions
2. **Maintenance Coordination** - Manual routing between tenants, vendors, owners
3. **Owner Reporting** - Monthly reports often compiled manually
4. **Lease Renewals** - Tracking and reminder process is manual

### Medium Priority
5. **Rent Collection** - Late payment follow-up is time-consuming
6. **Move-in/Move-out** - Scheduling and checklist coordination
7. **Tenant Screening** - Multi-step process with manual handoffs
