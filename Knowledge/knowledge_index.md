## 🏢 Industry Deep-Dives

### 1. Property Management Industry
**Why This Vertical:**
- Drowning in repetitive tasks (tenant inquiries, maintenance, reporting)
- Most use outdated software with manual gaps
- Clear ROI: "Save 15 hours/week managing 50 units"
- High willingness to pay (8-12% of rent = budget exists)

**Specific Pain Points:**
- Tenant inquiry response (nights/weekends)
- Maintenance request routing and follow-up
- Move-in/move-out scheduling
- Owner monthly reporting
- Lease renewal reminders

**Austin Companies to Target:**
- Rollingwood Management (since 1986 - legacy systems)
- Stone Oak Property Management
- ATX Property Management (boutique)
- 1836 Property Management (tech-forward)
- Texcen Realty (500+ properties)

**Discovery Questions:**
- "Walk me through what happens when a tenant submits a maintenance request at 10pm Saturday"
- "How do you handle lease renewal reminders?"
- "What does owner reporting look like? How long does it take?"
- "When's the last time a good tenant left because of slow response time?"

**Outreach Angle:**
> "I help Austin property managers eliminate 15+ hours/week of admin work—tenant inquiries, maintenance coordination, owner reporting—with automated systems. I just finished a project for a local service business that cut their response time from 24 hours to 5 minutes. Worth a 15-minute call?"

### 2. Med Spas / Aesthetic Clinics
**Why This Vertical:**
- High-ticket services ($200-2K+/visit) = budget for operations
- Appointment-heavy with complex follow-up needs
- Owners are clinicians, not operators (hate admin)
- Recurring clients need nurture (Botox every 3-4 months)

**Specific Pain Points:**
- Appointment booking/confirmation
- Treatment reminders (Botox at ~3 months)
- Post-treatment follow-up sequences
- Before/after photo collection
- Membership/package tracking
- Review requests

**Austin Companies:**
- Viva Day Spa (5 locations)
- Austin Med Spa (since 2004)
- Aesthetica Med Spa (since 1999)

---

### 3. Home Services (HVAC/Plumbing/Electrical)
**Why This Vertical:**
- Emergency-driven = chaotic operations
- Dispatch/scheduling/follow-up are manual nightmares
- Field techs don't update CRMs
- Seasonal spikes create backlogs
- High customer LTV ($10K+ over time)

**Specific Pain Points:**
- Emergency dispatch and routing
- Appointment scheduling/confirmation
- Service reminder campaigns (annual HVAC maintenance)
- Post-service follow-up and reviews
- Quote follow-up sequences
- Warranty tracking

**Austin Companies:**
- Abacus Plumbing, AC & Electrical
- Fox Service (50+ years)
- Stan's Heating/Air/Plumbing (since 1954)
- Radiant Plumbing & AC

## 📖 Discovery Question Library

### Universal Questions (All Industries)
1. "Walk me through a typical day/week. Where do you spend the most time?"
2. "What's the most frustrating repetitive task you do?"
3. "If you had a magic wand, what would you automate first?"
4. "How do you currently follow up with leads/clients?"
5. "What tools are you using now? What's working? What's not?"
6. "How much time do you spend on admin vs. revenue-generating work?"

### ROI Quantification
1. "How many [inquiries/leads/requests] do you get per week?"
2. "How long does each one take to handle?"
3. "What's your current response time?"
4. "How many fall through the cracks?"
5. "What does that cost you in lost revenue/time?"

### Decision-Making
1. "Have you tried to solve this before? What happened?"
2. "What would make this a no-brainer investment?"
3. "If we could save you [X hours/week], what would that be worth?"
4. "Who else needs to be involved in this decision?"

## 🧠 Key Learnings & Principles

### What Makes a Good Automation Client
✅ **Yes:**
- Spending >10 hrs/week on repetitive tasks
- Using multiple disconnected tools
- Measurable pain (hours, response time, conversion rate)
- Budget exists ($500-5K/month revenue per employee)
- Decision maker accessible

❌ **No:**
- "We need better marketing" (too vague)
- Broke or cheap (no budget)
- Complex approval process (slow decisions)
- No clear pain point

### Pricing Philosophy
- **Base:** $8,500 (Freedom Engine - full system build)
- **Upsell:** $500-1,500/month retainer (maintenance + optimization)
- **Quick wins:** $500-1,500 (for smaller projects)
- **Referral bonus:** $500 (after client closes)

**Decision Rule:** If ROI isn't 10x in first year, not a fit.
