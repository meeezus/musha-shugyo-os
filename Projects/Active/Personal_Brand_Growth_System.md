## The Daily System (Optimized for Hunter Brain)

### Morning (30 min total)

**Option A: Voice Memo Flow** (Preferred)
1. Walk with AirPods, capture observation (15 min)
2. Send voice memo to Claude session
3. Claude drafts 2 tweets using your structures
4. You edit/approve and schedule (10 min)
5. Quick engagement: 5 meaningful replies (5 min)

**Option B: Direct Draft Flow** (If no walk)
1. Review yesterday's notes (5 min)
2. Draft 2 tweets using structures below (15 min)
3. Schedule (5 min)
4. Engagement: 5 replies (5 min)

### During Day (Passive)
- Voice memo insights (health, business, training)
- Screenshot good content for inspiration
- Note resistance moments in PersonalOS

- Reply to comments
- Log wins in PersonalOS

## Newsletter Framework

### Structure (1,000-1,500 words)

**Hook (100-150 words)**
- Open with specific moment/story from your week
- Visceral — what did you feel?

**Story (300-400 words)**
- Expand the moment
- Include dialogue, internal thoughts
- Show the struggle or insight

**Lesson (300-400 words)**
- What did this teach you?
- Connect to larger principle (sovereignty/discipline/health)
- Why does it matter?

**Application (200-300 words)**
- Practical takeaway for reader
- How does this apply to their path?

**CTA (50-100 words)**
- Invite discussion
- Ask a question
- Remind to subscribe
