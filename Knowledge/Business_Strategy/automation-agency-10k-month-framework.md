Complete framework for launching an automation agency from zero to $10K/month in 90 days. Focuses on targeting underserved operations, pricing for business value, and using modern no-code tooling.

## Key Strategy Points

**Step 1: Build One Thing Well**
- Master one workflow type (e.g., lead enrichment)
- Refine it across 10 different client implementations
- Don't try to learn everything at once

**Step 2: Target Underserved Operations**
- Find businesses with obvious repetitive work
- Example: Local marketing agencies spending 8-12 hrs/week on manual client reporting
- Automation goldmine nobody is solving

**Step 3: Sell the Outcome First**
- Lead with "eliminate 8 hours of weekly reporting work"
- NOT "I build n8n automations"
- Sell time savings, not technology

**Step 4: Recommended Modern Tooling**
- n8n for backend logic
- Lovable for client-facing interfaces
- Claude for error handling and validation
- Turns 40-hour builds into 8-hour builds

**Step 5: Deliver Documentation**
- Workflow architecture diagram
- How to modify key parameters
- Troubleshooting guide
- Video walkthrough
- Enables clients to self-maintain or pay monthly retainer

**Step 6: Price for Business Value**
- Wrong: "automation setup - $500"
- Right: "eliminate 8hrs/week manual work - $6,800"
- Price for problem solved, not hours worked

## 90-Day Path

**Month 1:**
- Build signature workflow
- Refine 3-4 times
- Document the pattern

**Month 2:**
- Identify 20 businesses with the problem
- Cold outreach explaining time savings
- Land 1-2 clients at $6-8K each

**Month 3:**
- Deliver and document
- Ask for referrals
- Repeat with refined process

**Results:** $10K/month after 90 days, $30K/month by month 6
