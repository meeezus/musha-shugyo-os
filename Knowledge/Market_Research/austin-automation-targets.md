## Primary Target: Property Management

### Market Size (Austin Metro)
- **50+ property management companies**
- 10-15 major players (100+ units each)
- 30+ boutique firms (10-100 units)
- Growing market (Austin population +3% annually)

### Pain Points Identified

#### 1. Tenant Inquiry Response Time
- **Problem:** High volume of inquiries via phone/email/text/web forms
- **Current State:** Manual response, slow follow-up = lost leads
- **Automation Opportunity:** Auto-responses, lead capture forms, calendar booking
- **ROI:** Faster response = higher conversion to showings

#### 2. Maintenance Request Coordination
- **Problem:** Tenant requests → vendor assignment → status updates → completion
- **Current State:** Phone tag, spreadsheets, missed updates
- **Automation Opportunity:** Request forms, vendor routing, status notifications
- **ROI:** Happier tenants, less time spent coordinating

#### 3. Owner Reporting
- **Problem:** Monthly financials, occupancy reports, maintenance summaries
- **Current State:** Manual data compilation from multiple sources
- **Automation Opportunity:** Automated report generation from PMS (AppFolio, Buildium)
- **ROI:** Time savings, professional presentation

#### 4. Lease Renewals
- **Problem:** Manual outreach 60/90 days before expiration
- **Current State:** Spreadsheet tracking, manual emails
- **Automation Opportunity:** Automated renewal campaigns, e-signature workflows
- **ROI:** Higher renewal rates, less vacancy

#### 5. Showing Coordination
- **Problem:** Scheduling showings with prospects
- **Current State:** Back-and-forth emails/texts, manual calendar management
- **Automation Opportunity:** Self-scheduling tools, automated confirmations/reminders
- **ROI:** More showings booked, less admin time

### Existing Tools (Common in Industry)
- **PMS Platforms:** AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager, TenantCloud
- **Pain:** These tools exist but are underutilized (too complex, no custom workflows)
- **Opportunity:** Custom automation layers on top of existing PMS

### Target Company Profile
- **Size:** 10-100 units managed
- **Team:** 1-3 people (owner + assistant)
- **Revenue:** $50K-$500K/year
- **Pain Level:** High (drowning in manual work)
- **Budget:** $5K-$15K for automation solution (if ROI is clear)

## Backup Vertical #1: Med Spas

### Market Size (Austin)
- **30+ med spas** in metro area
- Growing industry (aesthetic procedures +15% annually)
- High average transaction value ($500-$5,000 per visit)

### Pain Points
1. **Appointment Booking:** Phone calls, Instagram DMs, website forms (high volume)
2. **Appointment Reminders:** No-shows cost $500+ per slot
3. **Post-Appointment Follow-Up:** Rebooking, product recommendations, reviews
4. **Lead Nurture:** Consultation requests → conversion to treatment
5. **Membership Management:** Monthly subscription tracking, renewals

### Automation Opportunities
- Self-scheduling with deposit capture
- Automated reminder sequences (SMS + email)
- Post-visit rebooking campaigns
- Consultation funnel (inquiry → book → show → convert)
- Membership renewal workflows

### Target Company Profile
- **Size:** 1-3 locations
- **Team:** 3-10 staff (owner, aestheticians, front desk)
- **Revenue:** $500K-$2M/year
- **Budget:** $8K-$15K (high-margin business, can afford tools)

## Discovery Question Library (All Verticals)

### Opening Questions (Understand Current State)
1. "Walk me through what happens when a new lead/inquiry comes in - what's your process?"
2. "What tools are you using right now to manage [bookings/appointments/tenants]?"
3. "How do you currently handle [maintenance/follow-up/scheduling]?"
4. "What does a typical day look like for you?"

### Pain Point Identification
5. "Where do you feel like you're spending the most manual time each week?"
6. "What's the most frustrating part of [property management/running a med spa/etc.]?"
7. "How often do you miss opportunities because you couldn't respond fast enough?"
8. "What happens when you're out of the office or on vacation?"

### Goal Setting
9. "If you could automate one thing tomorrow, what would it be?"
10. "What would having an extra 10 hours per week free up for you to do?"
11. "What's your goal for the business this year? (growth, profitability, time freedom?)"

### Decision-Making Context
12. "Have you looked at automation or CRM tools before?"
13. "What stopped you from implementing them?"
14. "What would make a solution worth investing in for your business?"
15. "Who else would need to be involved in making this decision?"

### Budget Qualification
16. "If a solution saved you 10 hours/week, what would that be worth to you?"
17. "Have you budgeted for tech/systems improvements this year?"
18. "What's your typical process for evaluating new tools?"
